The visual structure must be determined by:
Job
Profession

Industry
Artifacts
Do NOT make every portfolio a SaaS dashboard.
A technical engineering case study should look technical.
A Customer Success case study should look customer/business oriented.
A Sales Operations project should communicate revenue and operational intelligence.
A Product Design case study should emphasize product thinking and visual artifacts.
A Project Management case study should emphasize execution, dependencies, risk and delivery.
An AI Operations case study should emphasize systems, workflows and implementation.
The design must be role-native.
